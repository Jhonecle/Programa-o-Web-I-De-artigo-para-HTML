Abstract

This paper presents the IFBot, an artificial intelligence chatbot developed to optimize access to institutional information in an educational institution. The solution uses the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) approach, combining web scraping and analysis of public documents to provide automated and reliable responses.
